把「一个伟大的想法」写出高级感的表达手册

从 Unit 2 写作讲义与课文表达清单中，精挑出可直接落笔的高分整句、人物描述素材、亮点短语三组，按写作环节重新编排，每条标注语法亮点与使用位置。

字数 80–120 词 中考叙事类说明文 精选表达 48 条 点击任意表达一键复制

先立骨架：问题 — 想法 — 解决 — 影响

四个问题四步走，一段一问、不越级、不跳步。审题和结构都按这条链走。

Step 1 Problem 需要解决的问题是什么？先让读者感到痛。 a simple cut could lead to death
Step 2 Idea 谁提出了这个绝妙的想法？抓住那个"不寻常"的瞬间。 noticed something unusual
Step 3 Solution 问题是如何被解决的？写方法，也写试错与坚持。 kept testing different materials
Step 4 Influence 对生活产生了什么影响？落到人身上，收得住。 millions of lives have been saved

三段框架 × 时态 × 过渡语

时态本身就是得分点：过程用过去，影响用完成，结尾再回现在。

段落
核心内容
推荐时态
连接词 / 过渡语
开头 · 总起
引出伟大想法，点明它解决的核心问题
一般现在时
One of the greatest ideas that... Many great ideas have come from...
中间 · 分述
问题的表现 → 想法的产生 → 解决的具体方法
一般过去时
At that time... One day... To his surprise... With this idea in mind...
结尾 · 总结
总结想法的影响，说明它如何改变了生活
现在完成时 + 一般现在时
Since then... Thanks to this idea... Now it is common to... ... can still be seen today.

提分提示：结尾不要只用一个时态。先用 现在完成时（has been widely used / have been saved）拉开"从那以后到现在"的时间跨度，再用 一般现在时（it is common to / can still be seen today）把视角拉回今天。两种时态一对比，文章的"时间纵深"就出来了。

21 条高分整句 · 按写作环节分组

整句可直接搬进正文。标签是真正的语法得分点：倒装、强调句、名词性从句。

Opening开篇定调02
One of the greatest ideas that changed the world is the invention of penicillin. 改变世界的最伟大想法之一，是青霉素的发明。 定语从句开篇点题 · 最稳的起手式
Many great ideas have come from people's daily lives. 许多伟大的想法都来自人们的日常生活。 现在完成时 · 由大到小切入
Problem铺陈问题03
In 1928, Alexander Fleming was studying bacteria in his laboratory. 1928 年，亚历山大·弗莱明正在他的实验室里研究细菌。 过去进行时写"背景中的进行动作"
Before penicillin, a simple cut or insect bite could lead to serious infection and even death. 在青霉素出现之前，一个小小的伤口或虫咬，都可能导致严重感染，甚至死亡。 Before + could 弱化断言 · 不是事实判断而是当时的可能性
At that time, people had no way to treat bacterial diseases effectively. 那时，人们没有办法有效治疗细菌性疾病。 had no way to do sth. · 高频"无奈"表达
Discovery灵光时刻05
One day, Alexander Fleming noticed something unusual in his laboratory. 有一天，弗莱明在实验室里注意到一些不寻常的东西。 notice + adj. 宾语 · 悬念的起点
To his surprise, there were no bacteria around the mould. 令他惊讶的是，霉菌周围没有细菌。 to one's surprise · 开头独立成分
He realized that the mould was killing the bacteria. 他意识到，这种霉菌正在杀死细菌。 宾语从句 · 全文"想法成立"的转折核心
Fleming's discovery of penicillin shows that a great mind can notice scientific value in what others may simply dismiss as an accident. 弗莱明发现青霉素这件事表明：伟大的头脑，能在别人当作偶然的现象中看出科学价值。 ★ what 引导名词性从句 · 全篇格调最高的一句
Suddenly, I had a brilliant idea. 突然，我冒出了一个绝妙的主意。 写"自己的生活小事"时，人物一换直接可用
Solution攻克难关04
A useful idea often begins with observation, but it becomes convincing only when it is supported by clear reasoning. 一个有用的想法往往始于观察，但只有被清晰的推理支撑时，它才站得住脚。 but + only 双重递进 · 议论型句子，评卷老师一眼看到
If one method leads nowhere, a good problem-solver changes the angle instead of repeating. 一种方法走不通时，优秀的解题者会换一个角度，而不是原地重复。 ★ 条件句 + instead of · 写"试错与坚持"最好用的一句
Driven by intellectual curiosity, Edison kept testing different materials until electric lighting became practical for daily use. 出于对知识的好奇，爱迪生不断测试不同的材料，直到电灯变得可以日常使用。 过去分词作状语 + until 时间链 · 换个人名即可复用
A truly creative person is not only imaginative, but also careful enough to turn a bright idea into something useful. 真正有创造力的人，不仅想象力丰富，还足够细心，能把一个亮眼的点子变成有用的东西。 not only... but also... · 总结人物"为什么伟大"
Impact影响与升华07
Since then, antibiotics have been widely used to treat infections. 从那以后，抗生素被广泛用于治疗感染。 Since then + 现在完成时被动 · 结尾必备
Thanks to this accidental discovery, millions of lives have been saved. 多亏了这个意外的发现，数百万人的生命得以挽救。 accidental discovery 意外发现 · 点题"偶然中的伟大"
Not only did the idea solve the problem, but it also changed the way we live. 这个想法不仅解决了问题，还改变了我们的生活方式。 ★★★ Not only 倒装句 · 中考作文的"结构分"开关
It is diligence and curiosity that turn a small idea into a great invention. 正是勤奋与好奇，让一个小想法变成了伟大的发明。 ★★ 强调句 It is... that · 收尾升华首选
Never underestimate the power of a small idea. 切勿低估一个小想法的力量。 祈使句收尾 · 独立成段，掷地有声
A small idea can make a big difference. 一个小想法，可以带来巨大的改变。 对比结构 small / big · 写感悟时最稳
The influence of this idea can still be seen today. 这个想法的影响，直到今天依然可见。 情态动词被动 · 结尾最后一句

15 条描述素材 · 写人物与思维过程

这些是短语片段而非整句，专门用来描述"发明者是什么样的人""他怎么想问题"，需要自己组装进句子。

Traits of Great Minds 伟大者的品质

intellectual curiosity and keen observation求知欲强、目光敏锐
driven by curiosity rather than external pressure由好奇心驱动，而非外部压力
pursue precision instead of relying on rough guesses追求精确，而不是靠粗略的猜测
with the courage to question conventional ideas有勇气质疑公认观念
perseverance in the face of repeated failures面对一次次失败仍坚持不懈
an observant eye for details others may miss一双能捕捉他人忽略细节的眼睛
logical, creative, self-motivated, flexible and open-minded逻辑清晰、有创造力、自我驱动、灵活且开放
combine creative ideas with disciplined thinking把创意与严谨的思维结合起来

Ways of Thinking 思考与解决问题的方式

identify the real problem before looking for a solution先找准真正的问题，再找方法
look beyond the surface of a problem看到问题的表层之外
reason from evidence rather than appearances依据证据推理，而非凭表面现象
turn an accidental observation into scientific evidence把偶然的观察转化为科学证据
test an idea against evidence用证据检验一个想法
revise one's explanation when the evidence does not fit当证据对不上时，修正自己的解释
develop a solution through observation and reasoning通过观察与推理，逐步形成解决方案

怎么用：这些短语自带高级词汇（perseverance、evidence、disciplined），但不能单独成句。最省力的做法是套进 He was the kind of person who... 或 What made it work was that...，立刻变成漂亮的定语从句。

金句 · 用于标题与首尾呼应

写作时不必全用，但用一句在开头或结尾，整篇的格调会明显不同。

“

Daring ideas are like chessmen moved forward.
They may be beaten, but they may start a winning game.

大胆的想法就像向前推进的棋子。它们可能会被击败，但可能开启一场胜利的棋局。

— Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

需要是发明之母。· 写"问题如何催生想法"时最贴题

万能模板 + 范文标注

先照着模板写满三段，再把高分句替换进去——顺序反过来会卡壳。

// 开头 · 一般现在时
One of the greatest ideas that changed the world is (伟大想法/发明).
It has solved the problem of (解决的问题).

// 中间 · 一般过去时
At that time, (问题的具体表现). One day, (人物) (发现/想到了什么).
He/She realized that (核心想法). With this idea in mind, he/she (解决问题的方法).

// 结尾 · 现在完成时 + 一般现在时
Since then, (想法的影响). Thanks to this idea, (对生活的改变).
The influence of this idea can still be seen today.
Penicillin: An Accidental Discovery That Changed the World 约 110 词 · 符合 80–120 要求
1开头 · 点题一般现在时

One of the greatest ideas that changed the world is the discovery of penicillin. It has solved the problem of treating bacterial infections.

2中间 · 问题 → 发现 → 领悟一般过去时

Before penicillin, a simple cut or insect bite could lead to serious infection and even death. In 1928, Alexander Fleming was studying bacteria in his laboratory. When he came back from holiday, he noticed that mould was growing in some dishes. To his surprise, there were no bacteria around the mould. He realized that the mould was killing the bacteria. He called this substance penicillin.

3结尾 · 影响现在完成时

Since then, penicillin has been widely used to treat infections. Thanks to this accidental discovery, millions of lives have been saved.

Point 01

结构不越级

严格走完"问题—想法—解决—影响"四步，一段只答一个问题，不跳跃、不回头补充。

Point 02

时态有层次

中间段用过去时讲过程（was studying / noticed / realized），结尾用完成时讲影响（have been used / have been saved）。

Point 03

短语撑高级感

lead to / to his surprise / widely used / accidental discovery 四个词块，加上 Before... 与 Since then... 的时间对比。

变 A Brilliant Idea That Solved My Problem · 同一框架的记叙文迁移 80–120 词

Last month, I faced a big problem. My cat always scratched my new sofa, and I didn't know what to do.
I tried many ways to stop her, but nothing worked. One day, I remembered that cats don't like the smell of oranges. Suddenly, I had a brilliant idea. I made a spray with orange peel and water. Then I sprayed it on the sofa. To my surprise, the cat never scratched the sofa again!
This experience taught me that we should think creatively when we face problems. A small idea can make a big difference.

迁移要点：把"科学发现"换成"生活小事"，四步链条照旧 —— 遇到问题 → 反复失败（nothing worked）→ 灵光一闪（a brilliant idea）→ 验证成功 + 感悟。同一批短语可以整套复用。
