五个亮点句模版 · 填空即用

这五个结构基本能撑起一篇 80 词的范文。方括号里换成你的内容，整句就是高级表达。

1身份 + 定语从句 · 用来开篇

[Name] is often introduced as a/an + 身份 who [具体贡献], and is still remembered as [名词短语].

MODELclick to copy
Qian Xuesen is often introduced as a leading scientist who helped lay the foundation for China's space technology, and his name is still remembered across generations.
2成果 → 影响 · 用来写成就段落

[Name]'s research on [课题] helped [improve / solve] [现实问题], and showed how [a field] could respond to real needs.

MODELclick to copy
Yuan Longping's research on hybrid rice helped improve food production, and showed how science could respond to real needs.
3冒号 + 分词 · 用来写「影响深远」

[Name]'s [work] reshaped [field]: it changed the way [people] [understand X], opening up new directions for later research.

MODELclick to copy
Einstein's theories reshaped modern physics: they changed the way scientists understand space and time, opening up new directions for later research.
4小细节见大品质 · 最容易出彩

[Name]'s [quality] can be seen in a small but unforgettable detail: [一个具体动作].

MODELclick to copy
Chen Wangdao's dedication can be seen in a small but unforgettable detail: while translating The Communist Manifesto, he was so focused that he mistook ink for brown sugar sauce.
5名言收尾 · 结尾升华

Just as [Name] said, "…". This reminds me to [做什么], not just [successful], but [valuable].

MODELclick to copy
Just as Albert Einstein said, "Try not to become a man of success. Rather become a man of value." This reminds me to become not just successful, but someone who brings value to others.

避坑 · 五条

素材里有些句子能写但不出彩，有些则是翻译残留 —— 一起标出来。

能写但偏平的句子

have a great influence on later generations 没错，但太安全。优先用 reshape / open a new path / leave a lasting influence。

少用 very + 形容词

very important / very good 一律换掉：remarkable、significant、far-reaching、little-known。

别整段都是 because…so…

换一个句式呼吸：Instead of…, / With…, / Not only…but also… / 结尾用冒号 + 分词。

亮点句要限量

80 词短文放 1–2 句亮点句最稳，其余用简单句保准确率。炫技过多反而被挑语法错。

翻译残留，直接丢弃

加油包里的「这当人们谈到中国伟大的科学家时……」以及 have a great influence on later generations 这类直译句，中文一读就露馅，别当素材背。
